# 🎬 GigGuard Phase 1 Pitch Script
*Target Length: 2 Minutes (~280 words)*
*Format: Single Speaker / Solo Pitch directly to camera.*

---

## ⏱️ [0:00 - 0:25] THE STRATEGY & PROBLEM
**[Tone: Empathetic, story-driven]**

"Hi judges, we are Team GigGuard. 

Meet Rahul: one of India’s 1.2 crore gig workers delivering for Zomato. He works 12-hour shifts to net just 800 rupees a day. But last Tuesday at 7 PM, a massive cloudburst hit Mumbai. Rahul had to log off for his own safety. He lost his peak-hour earnings, his daily incentives, and his only source of income for the day. He has zero safety net. 

Our strategy is to fix this with **GigGuard**—an AI-powered parametric insurance platform."

---

## ⏱️ [0:25 - 0:50] THE BUSINESS MODEL
**[Tone: Confident, explaining the business model]**

"Traditional insurance takes weeks; GigGuard takes seconds. 

Because gig workers operate week-to-week, our strategy relies on a 3-tier Weekly Micro-Premium model starting at just 30 rupees a week. Our AI risk-engine dynamically prices this premium based on 10 years of meteorological data for Rahul’s specific delivery zone down to the municipal ward."

---

## ⏱️ [0:50 - 1:15] THE PROTOTYPE (Minimal Scope)
**[Tone: Excited, technical]**

"For our Phase 1 minimal prototype, we have modeled the core parametric logic. 

We integrated the OpenWeatherMap API and MapMyIndia traffic data. The moment rainfall exceeds our 40-millimeter threshold, and our data confirms severe road blockages in Rahul’s geo-fenced commercial zone, our backend logic executes an instant UPI payout directly into his wallet. No paperwork, no waiting."

---

## ⏱️ [1:15 - 1:40] ADVERSARIAL DEFENSE PIVOT
**[Tone: Authoritative. The "mic-drop" moment indicating you solved the 24-hr threat]**

"But to protect this system from GPS-spoofing fraud rings, simple GPS tracking is dead. 

Our prototype logic uses an Adversarial Defense architecture. We triangulate Cellular Tower IDs and scan local Wi-Fi MAC addresses. If an app says you're in a flooded market, but your network pings a residential tower 30 kilometers away, the claim is auto-rejected. If the network drops legitimately, we pause the payout and ask for a 3-second photo of the flooded street."

---

## ⏱️ [1:40 - 2:00] PLAN OF EXECUTION (Phase 2 & 3)
**[Tone: Strong, team-focused, forward-looking]**

"Our Plan of Execution is clear: 
In Phase 2, we are turning our Figma wireframes into a React Native mobile app and wiring these live API triggers. In Phase 3, we deploy the computer vision anti-fraud model and the Razorpay sandbox for instant payouts. 

We are Team GigGuard, and we are building the missing livelihood net. Thank you."
